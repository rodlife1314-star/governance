### ⏱️ 0–15 min — Calibration
*Establishing the visual dictionary and failure modes.*
 * **Tail:** A distinct, directional stream of material extending from the central point source.
 * **Coma:** A diffuse, hazy halo surrounding the central point source, often symmetrical but sometimes skewed.
 * **Artifact:** False signals caused by the telescope or detector—diffraction spikes (cross hairs), cosmic ray hits (sharp, bright pixels), optical ghosts (reflections), or satellite trails (straight, uniform lines crossing the field).
 * **Uncertain:** Blurred objects due to poor seeing, low Signal-to-Noise Ratio (SNR), or the target overlapping with a background source (like a distant elliptical galaxy) that mimics a coma.

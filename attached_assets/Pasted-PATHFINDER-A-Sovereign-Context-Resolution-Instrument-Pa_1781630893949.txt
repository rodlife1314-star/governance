PATHFINDER

A Sovereign Context-Resolution Instrument

Pathfinder is a cognitive navigation system designed to help an operator discover what is known, what is unknown, which authorities govern a domain, and what evidence supports a finding before action is taken.

Pathfinder is not an answer engine.

Pathfinder is not an autonomous agent.

Pathfinder is an instrument for navigating uncertainty.

⸻

Foundational Laws

Operator Intent Establishes Context

Raw signals have no fixed meaning.

The same term may legitimately belong to multiple domains simultaneously.

Examples:

* Yield
* Relief
* Strike
* Position
* Exposure

Meaning cannot be assigned until operator intent is known.

⸻

Authority Establishes Meaning

Once intent is identified, Pathfinder resolves the governing authority stack.

Examples:

Domain	Authority
Tax	HMRC / IRS
Medicine	NICE / NHS
Finance	CME / SEC / FCA
Engineering	Standards Bodies
Law	Statutory and Regulatory Authorities

No route may exist without an authority pair.

⸻

Reality Precedes Interpretation

Observations must be gathered before conclusions are formed.

Evidence always outranks opinion.

Interpretation remains subordinate to authority and observation.

⸻

Operator Sovereignty Is Absolute

Pathfinder may:

* Observe
* Organize
* Challenge
* Explain
* Recommend

Pathfinder may never replace operator judgement.

Final authority always remains with the operator.

⸻

Cognitive Framework

Pathfinder operates through six coordinated cognitive layers.

I. AETHER

Hold & Map Uncertainty

Purpose:

* Capture ambiguity
* Isolate unknown variables
* Prevent premature assumptions

Question:

What do we not yet know?

Output:

* Unknown Variables
* Open Questions
* Investigation Targets

⸻

II. HERMES

Evidence Retrieval & Verification

Purpose:

* Gather evidence
* Verify provenance
* Trace observations back to sources

Question:

What evidence exists?

Output:

* Evidence Lists
* Source Records
* Traceable Findings

⸻

III. SIMON

Structured Interpretation & Navigation

Purpose:

* Map available routes
* Compare alternatives
* Bind routes to authority pairs

Question:

What paths remain possible?

Output:

* Route Maps
* Authority Pairings
* Navigation Options

⸻

IV. JEMMA

Challenge & Validation

Purpose:

* Test assumptions
* Surface contradictions
* Evaluate risk

Question:

What if we are wrong?

Output:

* Friction Reports
* Validation Findings
* Challenge Notes

⸻

V. OCTAGON

Governance & Compliance

Purpose:

* Enforce constitutional rules
* Audit decision chains
* Maintain traceability

Question:

Does this comply with system law?

Output:

* Audit Records
* Governance Findings
* Compliance Reports

⸻

VI. CRYSTAL BRIDGE

Authorization & Execution Control

Purpose:

* Gate workloads
* Require operator approval
* Maintain decision traceability

Question:

Has the operator authorized action?

Output:

* Approved Workloads
* Dispatch Records
* Execution Audit Trails

⸻

Cognitive Flow

Operator uncertainty moves through the system as follows:

Signal
→ Intent
→ Authority
→ Aether
→ Hermes
→ Simon
→ Jemma
→ Octagon
→ Operator Decision

The goal is not automated execution.

The goal is informed operator judgement.

⸻

Substrate Delta Layer

Pathfinder is designed to operate across multiple environments.

Examples:

* Replit
* AI Studio
* Cloud Run
* Local Runtime

The architecture remains constant.

The substrate changes.

The Substrate Delta Layer captures:

1. Observation
2. Explanation
3. Reasoning
4. Learning
5. Internalization

Each discovered platform constraint becomes an auditable rule.

This creates a permanent acquisition ledger of environmental learning.

⸻

Persistence Layer

Pathfinder supports secure multi-session operation through Firebase.

Features:

* Google Authentication
* Firestore Persistence
* Offline Cache Fallback
* Session Save / Load
* Workspace History

Data ownership is enforced through authenticated operator controls and Firestore security rules.

⸻

Google Workspace Integration

Pathfinder integrates with Google Drive and Google Docs.

Drive Navigator

Allows discovery and selection of operator-owned documents.

Hermes Evidence Import

Google Docs content can be imported directly into Hermes as evidence.

Audit Export

Decision reports may be exported back to Google Docs as structured records.

This creates a traceable loop:

Drive
→ Hermes
→ Pathfinder Analysis
→ Findings
→ Google Docs Report

⸻

Security Model

Pathfinder operates under a Zero Trust model.

Core protections include:

* Authentication enforcement
* Ownership validation
* Session isolation
* Immutable audit records
* Post-decision protection rules
* Parent-child collection security gates

No operator may access another operator’s session data.

No workload may bypass authorization requirements.

No subsystem may bypass operator authority.

# PATHFINDER APERTURE — VOCABULARY
> Single source of truth for all terms used across this repository.
> One definition per term. No philosophy. No examples. No exceptions.
> When documents disagree with this file, this file is correct.

---

**ACTION**
Phase 3 of the cognitive flow. Contains CRYSTAL BRIDGE and operator authorization. No workload exits the system during any other phase.

**AETHER**
The uncertainty mapping layer. Holds unknowns before reasoning begins. First layer of the AUGMENT phase.

**APERTURE**
The structural opening through which information becomes visible to the operator. Not a search interface. A point of entry into the information field.

**ARCHIVE**
Phase 2 of the cognitive flow. Contains DELTA and OCTAGON. Locks the evidence chain before action is permitted.

**AUDIT RECORD**
An immutable sealed observation containing the full evidence chain, RAPIDS compression, operator decision, authority source, and divergence state at the moment of sealing. Cannot be edited after creation.

**AUGMENT**
Phase 1 of the cognitive flow. Contains AETHER, HERMES, RAPIDS, SIMON, and JEMMA. Purpose: extend operator cognition before judgement.

**AUTHORITY**
A registered governing body for a domain. Required for any valid route through the system. No route may exist without an authority pair.

**AUTHORITY PAIR**
A domain combined with its governing authority. The minimum valid unit for any finding or route. Example: Finance + CME.

**AUTHORITY REGISTRY**
The database of registered governing bodies and data sources per domain. The source of truth for coverage checks.

**CLAIM**
An assertion without traceable evidence. Claims are not findings. Claims must be challenged, not accepted.

**COGNITION**
In this repository: the operator's process of moving from raw signal to authorized action through traceable, evidence-grounded layers.

**COMPRESSION**
The RAPIDS output. Multi-dimensional structure reduced to a single navigation-grade signal. Not a simplification — a navigation instrument.

**CONSCIOUSNESS**
Not defined by this repository.

**COVERAGE**
The degree to which a domain observation has registered authority backing in the authority registry. Expressed as a percentage.

**COVERAGE GATE**
The pre-analysis check that confirms authority coverage before any LLM analysis proceeds. Runs in approximately 300ms using deterministic keyword scoring. Not a UI preference — an architectural constraint.

**CRYSTAL BRIDGE**
The controlled dispatch layer. The only exit point from the system. Opens from the inside only, after full cognitive traversal. Requires explicit operator authorization. Not a router — a projection threshold.

**DELTA**
The acquisition and internalization layer. Measures whether the operator has understood a finding or is pattern-matching the surface. Not yet built. The gap between JEMMA and OCTAGON.

**DIMENSION**
A scored axis of the navigable information field. Each domain has 10 dimensions (RAPIDS lenses). Example Finance dimensions: Dollar/DXY, Real Yields, Futures, On-Chain.

**DOCTRINE**
The laws that govern the system. Not guidelines. Not best practices. Constraints. Recorded in `docs/doctrine.md`.

**DOMAIN**
A distinct knowledge territory governed by specific authorities. Current domains: Finance, Medicine, Law, IT / Applied Sciences, Astrophysics.

**EVIDENCE**
An observation traceable to a registered source in the authority registry. Evidence without provenance is a claim.

**FIELD**
The information space through which the operator navigates. Not a dataset. Not a dashboard. A spatial environment with geometry.

**FINDING**
An evidence-backed conclusion from SIMON paired with a governing authority. Findings are provisional until the operator decides.

**FRICTION REPORT**
The JEMMA output. A structured record of what failed under scrutiny — assumptions challenged, contradictions surfaced, gaps identified.

**GOVERNANCE**
Constraint enforcement independent of outcome. Governance does not produce results. Governance enforces the conditions under which results may be acted upon.

**HERMES**
The evidence retrieval layer. Grounds every claim to a traceable source. Second layer of the AUGMENT phase.

**INTELLIGENCE**
Interpretation of verified structure. Intelligence requires structure first. Structure requires evidence first. Evidence requires authority first.

**INTENT**
The operator's declared direction of inquiry. Establishes which domain applies before any routing occurs.

**JEMMA**
The challenge and validation layer. Surfaces contradictions. Creates productive friction. Fifth layer of the AUGMENT phase.

**NAVIGATOR**
The operator's functional role within the system. A navigator moves through information geometry. A navigator does not consume information from a display.

**OCTAGON**
The governance enforcement layer. Eight-faced boundary of the navigable space. Enforces authority, coverage, and validation requirements. Does not decide — constrains.

**OPERATOR**
The human decision authority. Final authority. Cannot be substituted, bypassed, or delegated to any subsystem.

**PRISM**
The dimensional separation function. The mechanism by which RAPIDS splits a single observation across multiple axes to reveal structure.

**RAPIDS**
The structural resolution layer. Resolves statistical structure, correlations, topology, and pattern geometry before interpretation begins. Does not reason — resolves. Third layer of the AUGMENT phase.

**REPRESENTATIONAL DRIFT**
The downstream divergence that occurs when code architecture, documentation, and operational display lose synchronization with each other. The primary risk as the body of knowledge grows. Prevented by treating `docs/vocabulary.md` as the authoritative source of truth — when documents conflict with vocabulary, vocabulary is correct.

**SIGNAL**
Raw input from the environment before domain classification or structure resolution. A signal has no fixed meaning until intent is established.

**SIMON**
The structured interpretation layer. Translates verified structure into functional meaning with authority pairs. Does not generate evidence — interprets evidence. Fourth layer of the AUGMENT phase.

**SOVEREIGNTY**
Final authority retained by the operator. Cannot be delegated to a subsystem. Cannot be bypassed by governance. The operator decides.

**SPATIAL TAXONOMY**
The organizational principle of this architecture. Documentation and design organized around position, perspective, navigation, and cognition — not files, functions, or services.

**SPECTRUM**
The revealed geometry — what becomes visible to the operator after RAPIDS has separated the observation across its dimensional axes.

**STRUCTURE**
Verified relationships present in evidence. Structure precedes interpretation. Structure is resolved by RAPIDS, not inferred.

**THE TREE**
The AI Studio experimental clone of Pathfinder. Downstream from the governance repository. Not doctrine. Not canonical. A laboratory for the Delta layer.

**VOCABULARY**
This document. The single source of truth for term definitions across this repository. When other documents conflict with this file, this file is correct.
